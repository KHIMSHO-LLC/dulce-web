# Landing page (dulceglucosa.com, / and /en)

Scope: the single landing page that replaces every retired marketing route. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: Spanish-speaking T1Ds and their families decide whether Dulce is worth installing; the action is the App Store install (Android visitors: waitlist email).
Proof on hand: the product's own surfaces (Live Activity, alerts, StandBy, Watch, followers, logbook, trends), the founder's story, minute-by-minute sync. No user counts, testimonials or prices.
Constraints: correct the unsupported claims flagged in review; legal, /setup, /f/<code>, .well-known and /partners stay working; reduced motion and no-WebGL get static screens.
Memorable moment: one 3D iPhone carried down the page by scroll, turning to show each surface, docking for the night, handing off to the Watch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone is the page. One live 3D iPhone, its screen showing Dulce glucose ticking in real time, is carried by scroll through every place the number appears. Refuses the category default of a centred headline over a row of feature cards and floating flat mockups.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-black ground (#0D0C0B) crossed by a single diagonal ember light beam with grain and vignette. Ember (#F36638) only for brand and action. Glucose green/amber/crimson only where glucose data lives (screens, counter, range bar). Archivo variable: light (300) slightly wide for huge display lines, condensed 600 tabular for numerals. Dark glass surfaces with 1px hairlines, pill controls, one radius scale (pill controls, 28px cards).

STORY: Visitor sees their number already on a phone, understands it lives on Lock Screen, alerts, nightstand and wrist, sees who else can follow it, learns it is free with a Pro trial and made by a peer, installs from the floating pill or QR.

Centre-right a tilted 3D iPhone with a live glucose screen. Bottom-left a 20-word line, ember "Descargar gratis" pill and QR button. Bottom-right a glass card with founder line, official App Store badge and a Watch tile. Bottom-centre floating pill nav (menu, logo, Descargar). Ember beam behind the phone.
