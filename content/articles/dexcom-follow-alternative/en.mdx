If you wear a Dexcom, or follow someone who does, you've probably used Dexcom Follow and maybe Sugarmate. Both are free and both do their job. People usually start looking for something else for one reason: they want the number on the Lock Screen, the watch or the car without opening an app. This guide compares the three honestly, says where Dexcom Follow and Sugarmate are still the better choice, and shows how to try Dulce without changing anything you already use.

## Three apps, one source

All three read the same thing. Your Dexcom app uploads each reading to Dexcom, and with **Share** turned on Dexcom makes it available to other apps, a new reading every 5 minutes.

- **Dexcom Follow** is Dexcom's own app for **followers**: family members invited from the Share section of the Dexcom app.
- **Sugarmate** (now made by Tandem Diabetes Care) signs in with the Dexcom account and adds statistics, logging and extra ways to be alerted.
- **Dulce** also signs in with the wearer's Dexcom account and puts the reading where you already look: Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, widgets, Apple Watch and CarPlay.

Because they all read from Dexcom, you can use them side by side. Nothing changes on the sensor or in the Dexcom app.

## Where Dexcom Follow and Sugarmate are the better choice

Being honest here saves you time:

- **You follow several people, or many.** Dexcom Follow can follow up to 25 people in one app. In Dulce a follower follows one person at a time.
- **You need an alert that gets through silent mode.** Sugarmate can send critical alerts that override silent mode and Do Not Disturb, and can phone you. Dulce's alerts are regular notifications and follow your iPhone's sound settings.
- **You want detailed statistics and reports.** Sugarmate has far more numbers, a food database and report exports.
- **Everything should be free.** Both are free. Dulce's widgets, logbook and Trends are free, but the Live Activity, Apple Watch app and family sharing need Pro.

- **The number without opening anything.** A Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with the number, the trend arrow and the last hours, kept fresh by Dulce's server even with the app closed. With iOS 26 the same Live Activity shows on your car's CarPlay screen. See the <ArticleLink to="lock-screen">Lock Screen guide</ArticleLink>.

## Try Dulce without giving anything up

<Steps>
<Step title="Keep Share on">
In the Dexcom app, Share must be on with at least one follower. If someone already follows you in Dexcom Follow, that's enough.
</Step>
<Step title="Install Dulce and pick Dexcom">
On "Which sensor do you use?", tap **Dexcom**.
</Step>
<Step title="Sign in with the wearer's Dexcom account">
Use the account from the Dexcom app, not a Dexcom Follow follower's account, and pick the region where it was created: United States, Japan or Outside the US. The full walkthrough is in the <ArticleLink to="dexcom">Dexcom guide</ArticleLink>.
</Step>
</Steps>

Dexcom Follow and Sugarmate keep working exactly as before. If Dulce isn't for you, delete it and nothing else changes.

## For parents and followers

Dexcom Follow followers can't sign in to Dulce with their follower account: Dulce needs the account of the person wearing the sensor. There are two ways to follow someone with Dulce:

- **They use Dulce and invite you.** They need an iPhone and Dulce Pro; you install Dulce for free and open their link.
- **You sign in on your iPhone with their Dexcom account**, if you look after it (for example, you set it up for your child). Dulce then shows their readings on your Lock Screen and watch, and their phone doesn't change.

The <ArticleLink to="child-glucose">guide to following a family member's glucose</ArticleLink> explains both in more detail, with FreeStyle Libre as the example; with Dexcom the steps are the same.

<Callout type="warning">
Keep the Dexcom app's alarms on, and Dexcom Follow's if you're a follower. Dulce is not a medical device and doesn't replace them. Before acting on a reading, check its time and confirm with your sensor or your care team.
</Callout>
