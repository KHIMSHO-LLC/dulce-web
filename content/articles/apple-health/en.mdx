Apple Health is where your iPhone gathers health data from different apps and devices: steps, sleep, weight and, if you have diabetes, glucose, insulin and carbs too. The usual question is how your FreeStyle Libre fits in and what Dulce does with that data.

## What diabetes data Apple Health stores

Apple Health has categories for **blood glucose**, **insulin delivery** and **carbohydrates**. Each app writes what it wants, and you decide which apps may read and write each one. To see who writes your glucose, open the Health app, search for "Blood Glucose" and open **Data Sources & Access**.

Which apps write there depends on your sensor, your country and each app's version, so that screen is the most reliable place to check.

## What Dulce does with Apple Health

Dulce **only reads**. It never writes anything to Apple Health. It can read three things, and you choose which:

- **Glucose**: for example, readings from a Bluetooth meter that saves its data to Health.
- **Insulin**: for example, from a smart pen or another app where you log it.
- **Carbs**: for example, from a nutrition app.

That data appears in the <ArticleLink to="logbook">Logbook</ArticleLink> and in <ArticleLink to="trends">Trends</ArticleLink> next to your sensor readings, marked "from Apple Health". So what used to be spread across several apps sits in one place.

## How to turn it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the Apple Health settings in Dulce">
In Dulce, go to **Settings** and find the **Apple Health** section. You can also start from the "See your Apple Health data in Dulce" card in the Trends tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose what to read">
Turn on Glucose, Insulin or Carbs. It's a Dulce Pro feature, with a 14-day free trial.
</Step>
<Step title="Grant permission in iOS">
iOS shows its Health permission screen. Tick the categories you want to share with Dulce and tap **Allow**.
</Step>
</Steps>

In Settings you'll see when Dulce last read each category.

## How to revoke permission

You can revoke it any time from your iPhone: in the Health app, tap your profile picture, open **Apps** (or **Apps and Services**), choose Dulce and turn off what you don't want to share. You can also turn each category off in Dulce's settings.

## Privacy

Data Dulce reads from Apple Health is used to show it to you in the app. Dulce has no ads and doesn't sell data. The details are in our privacy policy.
