If your child, partner or one of your parents wears a FreeStyle Libre, you probably check on them more than you'd like to admit: a message at break time, a call when they don't answer, a look at the phone in the middle of the night. Seeing their glucose on your own iPhone, without asking, takes a lot of that weight off. This guide is for the person who follows, not the one who wears the sensor: what you need, the two ways to set it up, and how to keep it useful rather than stressful.

## What you need

- **A FreeStyle Libre 2 or Libre 3** on the person you care for, read by the Libre app on a phone (FreeStyle LibreLink or FreeStyle Libre 3). That phone has to stay with them and have internet, because that's how readings leave the sensor.
- **An iPhone for you.** Dulce is an iPhone and Apple Watch app.
- **Sharing turned on in LibreLinkUp**, Abbott's follower service. It's how readings reach any app other than the one that reads the sensor. The <PageLink to="/setup">LibreLink vs LibreLinkUp guide</PageLink> shows the four steps.

## Two ways to follow someone with Dulce

Which one fits depends on whose phone Dulce runs on.

### Option 1: they use Dulce and invite you

Best for teenagers and adults who have their own iPhone and want to stay in control of their data.

<Steps>
<Step title="They set up Dulce">
On their iPhone they install Dulce and sign in with the LibreLinkUp account that receives their readings.
</Step>
<Step title="They invite you">
In **Settings → Followers** they tap **Invite someone** and send you the link or the code. The invite expires after 24 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="You accept">
You install Dulce, which is free for followers, and open the link. With a code, tap "Following someone?" on Dulce's first screen and type it in.
</Step>
</Steps>

Sharing is a Dulce Pro feature for the person wearing the sensor, with a 14-day free trial. They can invite up to 3 people, remove anyone at any time, and you see their glucose from the moment you join. You don't need a sensor or an Abbott account.

### Option 2: you open Dulce with your own LibreLinkUp account

Best for younger children, or for an older parent who doesn't want another app on their phone.

If the Libre app on their phone already shares with **your** LibreLinkUp account, you can sign in to Dulce on your iPhone with that account. Dulce then shows the glucose shared with you, and nothing changes on their side. Dulce shows one person per LibreLinkUp account.

From there you can invite the other parent or the grandparents as followers yourself, as in option 1.

## What you see on your iPhone

- **Their glucose, the trend arrow and the chart**, with the time of the last reading so you know at a glance whether it's fresh.
- **Your own alerts** when they go low or high. As a follower you set your own quiet hours, and very low readings always come through.
- **Their glucose on your Lock Screen** with a Live Activity, so you don't need to open anything. How it works is in the <ArticleLink to="lock-screen">Lock Screen guide</ArticleLink>.
- **Short messages.** As a follower you can send "Are you OK?", "Check your glucose" or your own text. It arrives as a notification and they reply "I'm OK" with one tap, without opening the app. They can turn family messages off in Settings.

## At night

Nights are when following helps most. A few things make a difference:

- Keep your iPhone off silent at night, and if you use the Sleep Focus, allow Dulce in it (Settings → Focus). Dulce's alerts follow your iPhone's sound settings.
- Charge your iPhone on its side to use **StandBy** and keep the number next to the clock, as explained in the <ArticleLink to="widgets">widgets guide</ArticleLink>.

## Keep it calm

Following someone works best when you agree on it together. Some ideas that help:

- **Agree who answers an alert.** If two parents and a grandparent all react to the same low, the person with diabetes gets three calls instead of help.
- **Give it a moment.** A low alert doesn't always need a message. The trend arrow and the next reading often tell you whether they're already treating it.
- **Respect privacy with teenagers.** Option 1 lets them decide who follows and switch messages off. That trust is worth more than one extra reading.

<Callout type="warning">
Dulce is not a medical device and doesn't replace the Libre app's alarms, which the person wearing the sensor should keep on. Signal-loss alarms aren't shared through LibreLinkUp: if their phone loses the sensor or the internet, you'll just see the time of the last reading fall behind. Never make treatment decisions from a reading without checking its time; if in doubt, they should confirm with their sensor or a fingerstick.
</Callout>

## If you see no readings

Almost always the sensor phone isn't uploading: no internet, the Libre app closed by the system, or sharing switched off. The <ArticleLink to="librelinkup-not-working">LibreLinkUp troubleshooting guide</ArticleLink> covers every cause.
