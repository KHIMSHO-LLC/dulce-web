When someone at home lives with diabetes, everyone else lives with it too: the mum checking her phone at 3 a.m., the partner asking "are you OK?" in the middle of a meeting. Sharing glucose helps, as long as it doesn't turn into a stream of alarms and messages. This guide shows how to do it with Dulce and how it differs from following someone with LibreLinkUp.

## Two ways to share your Libre

**LibreLinkUp** is Abbott's follower app. The person wearing the sensor sends an invite from the connected-apps section of their Libre app, and every follower needs their own LibreView account, must be an adult, and must be in the same region. It works, but each family member has to create an Abbott account and learn another app.

**Dulce** uses your LibreLinkUp account behind the scenes and lets you invite **up to 3 people** with a link. They just install Dulce, which is free; they don't need a sensor or an Abbott account. Sharing is a Dulce Pro feature (with a 14-day free trial) for the person wearing the sensor; followers pay nothing.

**Wear a Dexcom?** It works the same way: Dulce uses your Dexcom account through Dexcom Share, and your family needs neither a Dexcom account nor Dexcom Follow. See the <ArticleLink to="dexcom">Dexcom guide</ArticleLink> and how Dulce compares with <ArticleLink to="dexcom-follow-alternative">Dexcom Follow and Sugarmate</ArticleLink>.

## How to invite a family member

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your followers">
In Dulce, go to **Settings → Followers** and tap **Invite someone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send the link or the code">
Choose **Share link** or **Share code** and send it over WhatsApp, iMessage or whatever you like. The invite expires after 24 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="Your family member installs Dulce and accepts">
Opening the link on their iPhone takes them straight to accepting. If they use the code, they tap "Following someone?" on Dulce's first screen and type it in.
</Step>
</Steps>

From then on they see your glucose in real time, read-only. Note that followers see your data **from when they join**: their chart fills in over time and they don't get your earlier history.

## What your family member can do

- **See your glucose** with the trend arrow and chart.
- **Get alerts** when you go low or high, with their own quiet hours. Very low readings always come through.
- **Have you on their Lock Screen** with a Live Activity, just like yours.
- **Send you a short message**: "Are you OK?", "Check your glucose" or one they write. It arrives as a notification and you reply "I'm OK" with one tap, without opening the app.

## You stay in control

In Settings you can turn off **Allow messages from family** if you'd rather not get them, and remove any follower whenever you want: they stop seeing your glucose immediately. Your family member can also stop following you from their app.

<Callout>
A tip from our own family: agree beforehand on who does what when an alert comes in. If everyone messages at once on every low, the alert ends up being more stress than help.
</Callout>

## If your family member sees nothing

It's almost always because no new readings are reaching LibreLinkUp: the sensor phone has no internet, the Libre app was closed by the system, or sharing is switched off. We go through it in <ArticleLink to="librelinkup-not-working">LibreLinkUp not working</ArticleLink>. To keep alerts useful rather than annoying, see the <ArticleLink to="alerts">alerts guide</ArticleLink> too.

With a Dexcom, check that the Dexcom app still has internet and that Share is on. Dexcom sends a reading every 5 minutes, so a reading a few minutes old is normal.
