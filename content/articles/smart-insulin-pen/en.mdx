Writing down every injection is the part of diabetes care most of us skip. You inject, you get on with your day, and two hours later you can't remember whether you took your long-acting or how many units went in at lunch. A smart insulin pen solves that by remembering for you, and Dulce reads that memory with your iPhone and puts every dose in your Logbook, right next to your glucose. It's free, and it takes a couple of seconds.

## What a smart insulin pen records

Novo Nordisk's **NovoPen 6** and **NovoPen Echo Plus** are reusable pens with a dose memory built in. According to Novo Nordisk, they record the time and date of every injection and the number of units, and keep the **last 800 doses**, which is roughly three months for most people. That includes the small safety shots you do before a dose.

| Pen | Dose range | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NovoPen 6 | 1 to 60 units | 1 unit |
| NovoPen Echo Plus | 0.5 to 30 units | 0.5 units |

The data stays in the pen until you hold it to a phone: the transfer works over NFC, the same contactless technology as Apple Pay. Novo Nordisk notes that the pens aren't approved in the United States; they're sold in Europe and other countries, usually through your diabetes clinic.

## Why it's worth using

- **Nothing to type, nothing forgotten.** Every dose arrives with the exact time and units the pen recorded. "Did I take my evening insulin?" gets a real answer: look at the Logbook.
- **Doses next to your glucose.** Each injection sits on the same timeline as your sensor readings, so you can see what happened after a meal and its bolus, or overnight after your long-acting.
- **Priming shots kept apart.** The small shot before each dose isn't counted as insulin you took (more on that below).
- **Better appointments.** Dulce's doctor report includes your logged insulin per day, so your care team sees doses and glucose together instead of a half-remembered notebook.
- **Free.** Reading a pen doesn't need Dulce Pro.

<Callout>
Dulce only records what your pen recorded. It doesn't calculate, suggest or check doses. Decisions about insulin are made with your care team.
</Callout>

## How to read your pen in Dulce

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the insulin entry">
Tap **+** to add an entry and choose **Insulin**, then tap **Scan insulin pen**. You can also do it from **Settings → Insulin pens**.
</Step>
<Step title="Hold the pen to the top of your iPhone">
Hold the top of your iPhone against the pen's label and keep both still for a couple of seconds, until Dulce says it's done. The NFC reader is at the top edge of the iPhone.
</Step>
<Step title="Tell Dulce what's in the pen (first time only)">
A pen doesn't know which insulin is in it, so the first time Dulce asks **What's in this pen?**: **Fast-acting** or **Long-acting**. You can change it later in Settings → Insulin pens.
</Step>
<Step title="Check your Logbook">
The doses appear in the **Logbook** marked **From pen**, and on today's chart. Scan again whenever you like: only new doses are added, never duplicates.
</Step>
</Steps>

## One-touch scan: hold the pen and Dulce opens

An iPhone can't open an app just because you hold an NFC tag to it, but Apple's Shortcuts app can, with an automation. Dulce walks you through it in **Settings → Insulin pens → Set up one-touch scan**: you copy Dulce's pen link, create an NFC automation in Shortcuts, scan your pen once, set it to run immediately and paste the link. It takes about two minutes, once per pen.

After that, you just hold the pen to the top of your iPhone: Dulce opens by itself and adds the new doses. No tapping.

## Priming shots and split doses

Before each injection you do a small safety shot to check the needle works. The pen remembers it like any other dose. Dulce treats small shots (**2 units or less**) just before a dose as priming and hides them, so your totals aren't inflated. If you want to see them, turn on **Show priming shots** in Settings → Insulin pens. Shots taken within a minute of each other are combined into one dose.

## Things to know

- **Which pens:** NovoPen 6 and NovoPen Echo Plus. Other connected pens and pen caps aren't supported yet.
- **Which iPhone:** any iPhone that runs Dulce has the NFC reader it needs.
- **Disposable pens** (the prefilled ones you throw away) have no memory, so there's nothing to read. You can still log those doses by hand in a couple of taps: see the <ArticleLink to="logbook">Logbook guide</ArticleLink>.
- **Long-acting and fast-acting** stay separate in the Logbook, and the <ArticleLink to="trends">Trends tab</ArticleLink> puts your glucose patterns right next to them.

<Callout type="warning">
The pen's memory is a record, not a safety check. If you're unsure whether a dose went in, don't inject again based on an app alone: check with your pen's instructions and your care team. Dulce is not a medical device.
</Callout>
