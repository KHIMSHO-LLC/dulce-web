If you drive, the moment you most want to know your glucose is often the moment you can't pick up your phone. There is no CarPlay app for Dulce, and you don't need one: since **iOS 26**, CarPlay can show an app's **Live Activity** on the Dashboard. Dulce's Live Activity has a small card made for exactly that place, with your number large enough to read at a glance.

This guide explains what you see, what you need, how to turn it on and which card to pick.

## How it works

On the iPhone, Dulce's Live Activity is the box with your glucose on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (see the <ArticleLink to="lock-screen">Lock Screen guide</ArticleLink>). With iOS 26, when your iPhone is connected to CarPlay, the same Live Activity appears on the **CarPlay Dashboard**. According to Apple, if the Dashboard isn't on screen at that moment, CarPlay shows it as a notification at the bottom of the display instead. Live Activities in CarPlay are read-only: you look, you don't tap.

CarPlay doesn't use the big Lock Screen design. It uses a **small card**, the same size Apple uses for Live Activities in the **Apple Watch Smart Stack**. iOS has one small card for both places, so whatever you choose for CarPlay also applies on your watch.

The card shows:

- **Your glucose and the trend arrow**, in large type.
- **The time of the reading.** When the reading starts to age, you see how many minutes ago it was taken instead. If it's too old, the number is greyed out, struck through and shown with a warning and an age that keeps counting, so an old number can't pass for a fresh one.
- Depending on the style: a **chart** of the last hours or your **time in range**.
- If you follow someone, **their name**, so you know whose glucose it is.

## What you need

- An **iPhone on iOS 26** and a car with **CarPlay** (wired or wireless). Dulce's widgets and Live Activity already need iOS 18; the CarPlay card needs iOS 26.
- **Dulce connected to your glucose.** A FreeStyle Libre 2, 2 Plus, 3 or 3 Plus through LibreLinkUp, a Dexcom G6, G7 or ONE+ through Dexcom Share, Medtronic CareLink or Nightscout: the card shows whatever reading reaches Dulce. The <PageLink to="/sensors">sensors page</PageLink> has the full list.
- **The Live Activity turned on.** It's a <PageLink to="/pro">Dulce Pro</PageLink> feature with a 14-day free trial. If you follow someone, your Live Activity runs on their Pro.

## Choose your CarPlay card

In Dulce, open **Settings → Live Activity style** and tap the **CarPlay** tab at the top. Under **CarPlay & Apple Watch** you'll find these styles, with a live preview above the list:

| Style | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Number and chart | The reading, the arrow and a small chart on a card coloured by your range |
| Big number | Only the reading and the arrow, as large as possible, on a card coloured by your range |
| Number and time in range | The reading with your time in range instead of a chart |
| Night (dark) | A dark card that is easier on the eyes at night |
| Match my Lock Screen design | The small version of your Lock Screen design (offered with Espresso, Porcelain, Aurora and Monolith) |

With a style that has a chart, you can also choose the **Time window** of that chart: 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours.

<Figure src="/guides/live-activity-styles.jpg" alt="Dulce's Live Activity style screen with a preview, next to an Apple Watch showing a small glucose card" caption="The Live Activity style screen (an earlier version: the current app has the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and CarPlay tabs at the top)." />

Which one to pick is personal. On a busy dashboard, **Big number** leaves the most room for the digits. **Number and chart** tells you at once whether you're heading up or down. **Night (dark)** avoids a bright coloured block on the screen when you drive in the dark.

## On your Apple Watch too

The same card appears at the top of the **Smart Stack** on an Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later while the Live Activity runs on your iPhone. That's separate from Dulce's own Apple Watch app and complications, which you can set up as described in the <ArticleLink to="apple-watch">Apple Watch guide</ArticleLink>.

## Limits worth knowing

- **Only while the Live Activity runs.** If iOS has ended it (Apple limits how long a Live Activity can stay active), the card disappears from CarPlay too. Open Dulce and it comes back.
- **The number is as fresh as its source.** With Dulce Pro, Dulce's server updates the Live Activity every minute for a Libre; Dexcom and Medtronic send a new reading every 5 minutes. If the phone that reads your sensor loses its connection, you'll see the reading's age go up.
- **Low Power Mode** cuts background updates.
- Without the Live Activity, there's no CarPlay card. The free <ArticleLink to="widgets">glucose widgets</ArticleLink> still put your number on the iPhone's Lock Screen and Home Screen.

<Callout type="warning">
Look at the card only when it's safe to, the same way you'd glance at your speed, and follow your local driving rules. The card doesn't replace your sensor's alarms or Dulce's alerts, and it is not a basis for treatment decisions. If you feel low, stop somewhere safe, confirm with your sensor or a fingerstick, and follow the plan you agreed with your care team. Dulce is not a medical device.
</Callout>
