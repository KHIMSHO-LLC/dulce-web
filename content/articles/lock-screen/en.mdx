We check our glucose dozens of times a day. If every check means unlocking the iPhone, finding the app and waiting for it to load, we end up checking less than we should. The official Libre app doesn't put your number on the Lock Screen, but the iPhone can do it with a **Live Activity**: a box that stays on the Lock Screen and also shows in the Dynamic Island.

<Figure src="/guides/lock-screen-alerts-watch.jpg" alt="iPhone Lock Screen with the Dulce Live Activity showing 120 mg/dL and glucose alerts below" caption="The Dulce Live Activity on the Lock Screen, with alerts underneath." />

## What you see on the Lock Screen

- **Your glucose and the trend arrow**, large.
- **The time of the last reading**, so you know at once whether the number is fresh.
- Depending on the style you pick, **the chart of the last few hours** or your **time in range**.

On iPhones with a **Dynamic Island** (iPhone 14 Pro and later) the number also sits at the top, next to the camera, while you use other apps. Touch and hold the island for the expanded view.

## How to turn it on

<Steps>
<Step title="Connect Dulce to LibreLinkUp">
If you haven't yet, follow the <PageLink to="/setup">LibreLinkUp setup guide</PageLink>. Dulce needs your LibreLinkUp account, not your LibreLink one.
</Step>
<Step title="Turn on the Live Activity">
In Dulce, go to **Settings** and turn on **Live Activity & Dynamic Island**. It's a Dulce Pro feature: turning it on lets you start the 14-day free trial.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow Live Activities in iOS">
In the iPhone **Settings** app, open **Dulce** and check that **Live Activities** is allowed. While you're there, turn on **More Frequent Updates** too.
</Step>
</Steps>

Lock your iPhone and you should see your glucose within seconds.

In **Settings → Live Activity style** you can change the look without touching anything else:

The graph styles help when you want to see where you're heading; Large and Compact when you only want the number.

## How often it updates

With Dulce Pro, Dulce's server updates the Live Activity every minute, even with the iPhone locked and the app closed. How fresh the number is depends on the Libre app on the sensor phone having uploaded the reading: if that phone loses internet, you'll see the time of the last reading fall behind.

## Does it work with the FreeStyle Libre 3 Plus?

Yes, the same way it works with the Libre 2, 2 Plus and 3. Dulce never talks to the sensor itself: it reads your glucose from **LibreLinkUp**, Abbott's sharing service. The sensor model doesn't matter, only that the app reading it shares to LibreLinkUp.

- **Which app reads the 3 Plus.** On iPhone that's Abbott's own app: FreeStyle Libre 3, or in some countries the newer **Libre** app, which Abbott sells as one app for any Libre sensor. Both share through LibreView and LibreLinkUp. Turn sharing on there, then sign in to Dulce with that LibreLinkUp account (the <PageLink to="/setup">setup guide</PageLink> has every step).
- **Compatible phones.** The phone that reads the sensor must be on Abbott's compatibility list for your country, which you'll find on your country's FreeStyle Libre website. Abbott updates that list as new iPhones and iOS versions come out, so check it before a big iOS update. Dulce has its own, separate requirement: iOS 17 or later.
- **Same iPhone or another one.** Because Dulce reads from LibreLinkUp and not from the sensor, it can run on the iPhone that reads your 3 Plus or on someone else's, like a parent's. See <ArticleLink to="family-sharing">sharing your glucose with family</ArticleLink>.

According to Abbott, the 3 Plus lasts **15 days** (one more than the Libre 3) and sends a reading **every minute**. That pairs well with the Live Activity, which Dulce Pro updates every minute. To know the exact day and time to change it, use the <PageLink to="/tools/sensor-end-date">sensor end date calculator</PageLink>.

## iOS limits worth knowing

- **iOS limits how long a Live Activity can run.** Apple sets a maximum number of active hours, then the system removes it. If it disappears, open Dulce and it comes back. If Dulce can't update your Lock Screen for a while, it sends you a quiet notification to open the app.
- **Low Power Mode** cuts background updates.
- **Live Activities don't appear on iPad.**

<Callout type="warning">
The Live Activity is a glance aid. Always look at the time of the reading, and confirm with your sensor's app or a fingerstick before treating a low or a high.
</Callout>
