Yes, if your readings reach your own Nightscout site, Dulce can show them on your iPhone Lock Screen, in widgets and on your Apple Watch. You give Dulce the https address of your site and, if the site is not open, a read-only access token. Dulce only reads glucose: it never writes to your site and ignores treatments.

<Callout>
Dulce is an independent app and is not a medical device. It doesn't replace your sensor's alarms or your uploader's alarms. Keep them on.
</Callout>

## Who this is for

Nightscout is a site you or someone you trust runs, fed by an uploader from your sensor or system. If you already open your readings in a browser, Dulce can read the same data and put it where you look: the Lock Screen, widgets and your wrist. It works with any sensor or uploader that feeds your Nightscout.

How often Dulce can update depends on how often your site receives readings. If your uploader sends one every 5 minutes, that is the rhythm.

## What you need

- A Nightscout site reachable on the internet with an **https** address. Dulce only connects over https, and addresses that only work inside your home network do not work.
- An **access token** if your site is not open. Create one in Nightscout under Admin Tools with the "readable" role. A read-only token is safer than your API secret.
- Recent readings: your site needs glucose readings from the last 24 hours.

## Connect Nightscout in Dulce

<Steps>
<Step title="Make a read-only token">
In Nightscout, open Admin Tools and create an access token with the "readable" role. Skip this if your site is open.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Nightscout in Dulce">
Download Dulce from the App Store. On "Which sensor do you use?", tap **Nightscout**. Enter the https address you open your site with, for example yoursite.herokuapp.com.
</Step>
<Step title="Paste the token and connect">
Pick **Access token** and paste it, or pick **API secret** if you want to use that instead. Dulce keeps only the SHA-1 fingerprint of the API secret, which is what Nightscout checks. Then tap **Connect**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Dulce never writes to your site. The address and token are kept encrypted on your phone and on Dulce's server, which keeps your Lock Screen up to date.

## Limits to know

- **Widgets can lag.** For FreeStyle Libre and Dexcom, Dulce's widgets can fetch a reading on their own. For Nightscout they cannot yet, so they show what the app and Dulce's pushes have already delivered, and iOS decides when they refresh. They always show how old the reading is. For a number that follows every reading, use the Live Activity.
- **Dulce can only be as fresh as your site.** If your uploader stops, nothing new reaches Dulce.
- **Check the time of the reading** before you rely on any number, and confirm with your sensor or a fingerstick when in doubt.

If Dulce says "Access denied", your site needs an access token or the one you entered is wrong. "Connected, but no readings" means the site has no glucose from the last 24 hours, so check your uploader. More on the <PageLink to="/sensors">sensors page</PageLink>. Using a Libre or Dexcom?
