There are three ways to see your glucose on Apple Watch, and each is good at something different. You can pick one or mix them. Most people end up with the **complication** on their watch face and the **Smart Stack** card for the moments they want every reading.

## Before you start

- Apple Watch is part of **Dulce Pro** (you can try it free for 14 days).
- On your iPhone, open Dulce and go to <Path>Settings › Lock Screen and widgets</Path>. Turn on **Apple Watch**.
- Dulce installs on the watch by itself. If it doesn't, open the **Watch** app on your iPhone, scroll to **Available Apps** and tap **Install** next to Dulce.

<Option n={1} title="Complication on your watch face" pick>

A small Dulce block that sits on your watch face next to the time, the weather or your rings. There are four shapes: **rectangular** (number, arrow and a chart of the last hours), **circular**, **corner** and **inline**.

To add it: touch and hold the watch face, tap **Edit**, swipe to the complications, tap a spot and choose **Dulce**.

<Figure src="/help/watch-complication.webp" alt="Dulce rectangular complication on Apple Watch showing 89 mg/dL, a trend arrow and a chart" caption="The rectangular complication: number, arrow and the last few hours." width={620} height={940} bare />

<Pros>
- Always on the watch face: just raise your wrist.
- Fits next to the rest of your face, and takes little room.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- watchOS decides when the face is redrawn, to save battery, so it can be a few minutes behind. A timer counts down to the next reading, and the complication says **old data** when the reading is too old: glance at it before you act.
</Cons>

</Option>

<Option n={2} title="Smart Stack (Live Activity)">

On watchOS 11 or later, the Dulce Live Activity from your iPhone also appears on the watch, in the **Smart Stack**. Turn the Digital Crown or swipe up from the watch face to see it. It updates with every new reading, just like your Lock Screen.

<Figure src="/help/watch-smart-stack.webp" alt="Dulce Live Activity in the Apple Watch Smart Stack showing 113 mg/dL, a trend arrow and a 24-hour chart" caption="The Smart Stack card: number, arrow and the last 24 hours." width={620} height={940} bare />

It needs the Live Activity on: in Dulce, <Path>Settings › Lock Screen and widgets › Live Activity & Dynamic Island</Path>. You can choose how the card looks in <Path>Live Activity style</Path>, under the **CarPlay** tab (iOS uses the same card for CarPlay and the watch).

<Pros>
- Updates almost in real time.
- Nothing to set up on the watch.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- It isn't on the watch face itself: one more turn of the crown or swipe.
- Only while the Live Activity is running on your iPhone.
</Cons>

</Option>

<Option n={3} title="The Dulce app on your watch">

Open Dulce on the watch (or tap the complication) for the big number, the trend arrow, the change since the last reading and a chart you can widen to the whole day. The refresh button asks for the latest reading.

<Figure src="/help/watch-app.webp" alt="Dulce app on Apple Watch showing 89 mg/dL, +2, a 12-hour chart and 100% in range" caption="The watch app: number, change, chart and time in range." width={620} height={940} bare />

Want it to stay on screen, for example during sport? On the watch, open **Settings › General › Return to Clock**, choose **Dulce** and turn on **Return to App**. You can also set the time to **After 1 hour**.

<Pros>
- The most information, always up to date while it's open.
- Can stay on screen for up to an hour.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- You have to open it to see your number.
</Cons>

</Option>

## And alerts?

Alerts come from your iPhone. When the iPhone is locked and you're wearing the watch, iOS sends them to your wrist. You set them up in <HelpLink to="alerts">alerts</HelpLink>.

If the number on your watch stops changing, see <HelpLink to="not-updating">readings not updating</HelpLink>.
