With family sharing, as many people as you like can see your glucose live on their own iPhone: a parent, a partner, a friend. It's part of **Dulce Pro**, and the people who follow you don't need Pro themselves.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Family sharing">
In Dulce, go to <Path>Settings › Family sharing</Path> and tap **Invite someone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Send a link or a code">
Choose **Share link** to send it by message, or **Share code** if they'll type it in. Invites work for 24 hours.
</Step>
<Step title="They open it">
They install Dulce on their iPhone and tap your link. With a code, they choose **Follow someone** and type it in.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Option title="Share link" pick>

<Pros>
- One tap for them: the link opens Dulce and connects.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- Has to be opened on the iPhone where Dulce is installed.
</Cons>

</Option>

<Option title="Share code">

<Pros>
- Easy to read out over the phone or copy by hand.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- They have to type it in Dulce themselves.
</Cons>

</Option>

## What your followers see

- Your number, arrow and chart. The chart fills in from the moment they start following.
- If they want: **alerts** when you go low or high, your number on **their Lock Screen**, and their own **quiet hours** (very low alerts always come through).

You can see who follows you in <Path>Settings › Family sharing</Path>. To stop someone, tap their options and choose **Remove**. They stop seeing your glucose at once.

## Messages

A follower can send you a quick message, like "Are you OK?". It arrives as a notification and you can answer from it with one tap. You can also write once to everyone who follows you, for example "I'm low, treating it". Messages aren't saved anywhere.

## If you sign out

Signing out of Dulce pauses sharing: your followers see that you signed out, and it starts again by itself when you sign back in with the same account.
