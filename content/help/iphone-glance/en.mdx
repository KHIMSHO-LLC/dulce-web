You don't have to open Dulce to know your number. The iPhone gives you three places to show it. Our pick is the **Live Activity**, because it's the only one that updates with every reading while your iPhone is locked.

<Option n={1} title="Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (Live Activity)" pick>

Your number, arrow and chart on the Lock Screen. On iPhone 14 Pro and later it also sits in the **Dynamic Island** while you use other apps, and it shows on the CarPlay Dashboard and the Apple Watch Smart Stack.

<Figure src="/guides/lock-screen-alerts-watch.jpg" alt="iPhone Lock Screen with the Dulce Live Activity showing 120 mg/dL" caption="The Live Activity on the Lock Screen." />

<Steps>
<Step title="Turn it on">
In Dulce, open <Path>Settings › Lock Screen and widgets</Path> and turn on **Live Activity & Dynamic Island**. Notifications must be on in <Path>Settings › Alerts</Path>.
</Step>
<Step title="Allow it in iOS">
In the iPhone **Settings** app, open **Dulce** and check that **Live Activities** is on.
</Step>
<Step title="Pick a look">
In <Path>Live Activity style</Path> choose a design. Some can be customised: background, colours, chart and emoji. The tabs at the top set the Dynamic Island and the CarPlay / Apple Watch card.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Pros>
- Updates with every new reading, even with the iPhone locked and Dulce closed.
- Also in the Dynamic Island, CarPlay and the Apple Watch Smart Stack.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- Part of Dulce Pro (14-day free trial).
- iOS ends every Live Activity after some hours. Dulce starts a new one by itself; if it ever goes missing, open Dulce once.
</Cons>

</Option>

<Option n={2} title="Widgets on the Home Screen and Lock Screen">

Two widgets: **Glucose** (number, arrow and how long ago) and **Glucose chart** (the last hours as a line). Small and medium on the Home Screen; rectangular, circular and inline on the Lock Screen.

To add one: touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Add Widget**, and search for **Dulce**. For the Lock Screen: touch and hold it, tap **Customize**, then the row under the time.

<Pros>
- Free.
- Always there, next to your other widgets.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- iOS decides when widgets refresh, so a widget can be a few minutes behind. Each one shows how old the reading is and has a refresh button.
</Cons>

</Option>

<Option n={3} title="StandBy on the nightstand">

Charge your iPhone locked and on its side, and it becomes a bedside clock. The Live Activity, or a Dulce widget, shows large next to the time. Good for checking at night without picking up the phone.

<Pros>
- Readable from bed, no touching.
</Pros>

<Cons>
- Only while charging in landscape. StandBy must be on in the iPhone settings.
</Cons>

</Option>
