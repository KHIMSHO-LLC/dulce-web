## How long a FreeStyle Libre sensor lasts

| Sensor | Wear time |
| --- | --- |
| FreeStyle Libre 2 | 14 days |
| FreeStyle Libre 3 | 14 days |
| FreeStyle Libre 2 Plus | 15 days |
| FreeStyle Libre 3 Plus | 15 days |

The sensor ends at the same time of day you started it: a Libre 3 started on a Monday at 8:00 ends two weeks later, on the Monday at 8:00. The calculator above uses your phone's time zone.

## The first hour: warm-up

Every sensor needs **60 minutes** to warm up before it gives readings. During that hour LibreLinkUp can keep showing the last value of your previous sensor, which looks live but isn't. Dulce detects the warm-up and shows a countdown on the Lock Screen and in widgets instead of a stale number; the <ArticleLink to="lock-screen">Lock Screen guide</ArticleLink> shows where.

<Callout>
Plan the change for a moment when an hour without readings is easy, not right before bed or a long drive. Keep a fingerstick meter at hand for that hour.
</Callout>

## When readings stop early

A sensor can stop before its end date: it came loose, got knocked, or the Libre app reports a sensor error. If readings stop but the sensor isn't due yet, first check that new readings reach LibreLinkUp at all with the <ArticleLink to="librelinkup-not-working">LibreLinkUp troubleshooting guide</ArticleLink>, then follow Abbott's instructions for a replacement.

## See days left in Dulce

Dulce shows how many days your current sensor has left on the home screen, so you don't need to count.
